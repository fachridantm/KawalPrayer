✨ Features

Everything you need,
right in your menu bar.

KawalPrayer runs silently in the background — no Dock icon, no ⌘Tab clutter. Just prayer times, always a click away.

🕌

Kemenag RI Accurate

Astronomical prayer time calculations following Indonesia's Ministry of Religious Affairs with +2 minute Ihtiyat safety margin. Supports MWL, ISNA, Umm al-Qura, and more.

📍

Auto Location Detection

Low-power GPS with 3 km distance filter. Shows your city & district (e.g. "Central Jakarta, Gambir") with persistent caching for instant display on launch.

🔔

Dynamic Island Alerts

Sleek notch-hugging prayer alerts that expand from your Mac's notch. Non-intrusive yet impossible to miss, with Stop Adzan and Dismiss controls.

📿

Auto Dua & Iqamah Timer

Post-Adzan dua pops up automatically with Arabic text, transliteration, and translation. Configurable Iqamah countdown (1–30 min) with notification.

🧭

Real-Time Qibla Compass

Great Circle spherical trigonometry for precise bearing to the Kaaba (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E). Visual compass with cardinal direction readout.

🎨

5 Menu Bar Formats

Choose between Icon + Name + Countdown, Icon + Countdown, Icon + Prayer Name, Icon + Prayer Time, or Icon Only. Dynamic icons match the current prayer.

🌍

Multi-Language

Full localization in Bahasa Indonesia, English, and Arabic with RTL support. Islamic Hijri calendar with configurable ±2 day offset.

🔄

Auto Updates

Built-in updater checks GitHub Releases automatically. Shows update banner in the menu bar popover with download progress and one-click install.

⚙️

Highly Customizable

Theme selection (System/Light/Dark), per-prayer notification mute, Adzan audio choice (Makkah/Madinah), calculation method, madhab, and launch-at-login.

⬇️ Download

Get KawalPrayer for free.

Download the latest release from GitHub. KawalPrayer is 100% free and open source under the MIT License.

1 Download from GitHub

  1. Go to the Latest Release page on GitHub.
  2. Under Assets, click on KawalPrayer-v*.dmg to download the disk image.
  3. Open the downloaded .dmg file.
  4. Drag KawalPrayer.app into your /Applications folder.

2 Launch the App

  1. Open KawalPrayer from /Applications or Spotlight (⌘ Space).
  2. The app will appear in your menu bar (top-right of screen).
  3. Grant Location and Notification permissions when prompted.
  4. That's it! Prayer times update automatically based on your location.

🛡️ Installation Guide

Bypassing macOS Gatekeeper

KawalPrayer is ad-hoc signed without a paid Apple Developer ID. macOS Gatekeeper may block it on first launch. Here's how to allow it safely.

1

Try opening the app

Double-click KawalPrayer.app in /Applications. macOS will show a warning dialog saying "KawalPrayer cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified". Click Done or Cancel — this registers the app with Gatekeeper.

2

Choose your method

Pick whichever approach you prefer:

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to the Security section.
  3. You'll see a message about KawalPrayer being blocked.
  4. Click "Open Anyway".
  5. Enter your password or use Touch ID to confirm.
  6. Click "Open" on the final confirmation dialog.

Run this command in Terminal to clear the macOS quarantine attribute:

$ xattr -cr /Applications/KawalPrayer.app

Then open KawalPrayer.app normally from /Applications or Spotlight.

3

You're all set! 🎉

KawalPrayer will now launch normally. Look for the prayer icon in your menu bar (top-right corner). The app only needs to bypass Gatekeeper once — subsequent launches will work without any extra steps.

Why is this needed? KawalPrayer is a free, open-source app. Apple charges \$99/year for a Developer ID certificate to sign apps. Since KawalPrayer is a community project, it uses ad-hoc signing — it's completely safe, and the full source code is available on GitHub for review.
